Bleed is exact zero, not a tolerance. Normalization multiplies every component by one scalar, and zero times any finite scalar is exactly zero. A tolerance here would hide a stray write.

The convolution control reproduces F2's 1/√2 saturation. F2 is therefore a live test rather than a memory. Two binding algebras exist and are separable by measurement: circular convolution for continuous waves, and modular addition in Z256 for discrete tokens. G-ING4's ≥ 0.9999 is provable only in the ring, where unbinding is exact modular subtraction.

F10 — the specification's incommensurate-frequency requirement is unsatisfiable. The spec asks for a frequency ratio not in ℚ. On a discrete torus that is impossible: exact G-ING1 covariance requires exp(i2πux) periodic in x with period 1, which holds if and only if u is an integer. Measured both ways: fractional frequencies give a residual near 1e-1 and fail the gate; integer frequencies give 2.589e-15 and pass. v6 uses a distinct integer lattice and reports its realized collinearity (16384 distinct pairs, 10043 primitive, 6341 collinear) instead of claiming a property the torus cannot have.

  1. The homodyne statistic is not new. Measured max difference against the existing sagnac_delta: 0.000e+00 on unit-modulus waves. Scope limit: off unit modulus they diverge — Stiefel 5.6e-02 versus tau_veto 4.31e-02. The statistic can flip a veto decision.

Two defects found in henri/memory.py, both measured before repair. write() was a total no-op that reported success: it scaled by 1/||KHk||², which is zero on a fresh store, so both factors stayed zero forever while updates incremented. The contract is a launch-count budget, not a FLOP budget. The shipped chain issues about eight kernels. 8 × 16.34 µs = 131 µs, which matches the measured 132.34 µs amortized cost to 1%. At D=65536 the data volume is 512 KiB per tensor, so a fused kernel would be bandwidth-bound at roughly 3 µs. Meeting 50 µs therefore requires at most three kernel launches, or CUDA Graphs. Statistic-form pinning — ratified, with a correction the probe could not see

Ratification, with one change of direction. One tau suffices: every family crosses at sqrt(2*tau) = 0.2936, measured 0.2967 in a fresh full-D run (receipts/blackwell_5090 sibling receipt, sha256 2ad0c623…). But the register proposed pinning the phase-mean form as authoritative. Measurement says the opposite: the phase-mean form is the one that is not invariant.

A dimension where c_d = 0 has angle(0) = 0, so cos = 1, so it contributes zero stress while still counting in the denominator. Constraint C6 mandates exactly zeroed unused slots. The phase-mean form is therefore diluted by D / n_active.

The veto crossing moves from 0.2936 to 0.5872 when three slots are zeroed: a doubling of tolerated phase corruption, on a state shape the constraint system requires. The divergence is exactly D/n_active in every row, so it is structural, not noise.

Why the seven-family probe could not find this. All seven families are dense, so n_active = D and the two forms cross within 0.0002 in eps. The sensor divergence of 1.231e-01 is a curve maximum at large eps, not a crossing difference. A probe whose families cannot be sparse cannot detect a sparsity defect. henri/form_pin.py now evaluates the pin on the state, not on the family label, and the gate reports the dilution factor it applied instead of hiding it.

The dual form is algebraically exact and mandatory: a D×D Gram at D = 65536 is 32 GiB in complex64 and 64 GiB in complex128, and the latter cannot be allocated at all on a 32 GiB card. Static memory scales as 16 M² bytes, so M=8192 costs 1 GiB and M=D costs the same as the dense form. The two costs cross where 9M³ = 2M²D, that is M ≈ 2D/9 ≈ 14.5k: below that the Gram product dominates, above it the eigen-solve does.

The “< 6 ms” claim is wrong by 127×. Measured 761.5 ms at M=4096. Newton-Schulz on the same operand measured 1007.0 ms over 15 iterations, so it is slower than eigh here, not faster. Any design that puts retraction inside a 50 µs loop is impossible on this arithmetic; retraction must stay on the cold path.

Measured copy bandwidth on the card: 2846 GB/s read+write (4 GiB in 2.81 ms), i.e. about 1.42 TB/s each way. At B=256 in complex64 the buffer streams 377 µs per tick, 7.5× the 50 µs contract, before any arithmetic.

Residency is impossible at B=256 for a second reason. The pinned invariant projector is 32 MiB and the FP8 codebook at M=8192 is 512 MiB. FP8 delay buffer 64 MiB + projector 32 MiB = 96 MiB, exactly 100% of L2, leaving zero bytes for the codebook or any other resident stream. The largest B that fits complex64 history plus the pinned projector is B_active = 32, eight times smaller than planned. The honest options are: keep history for Sagnac-stressed workers only, or store the low-rank delta instead of four full snapshots.
