Statistic-form pinning — ratified, with a correction the probe could not see

Ratification, with one change of direction. One tau suffices: every family crosses at sqrt(2*tau) = 0.2936, measured 0.2967 in a fresh full-D run (receipts/blackwell_5090 sibling receipt, sha256 2ad0c623…). But the register proposed pinning the phase-mean form as authoritative. Measurement says the opposite: the phase-mean form is the one that is not invariant.

A dimension where c_d = 0 has angle(0) = 0, so cos = 1, so it contributes zero stress while still counting in the denominator. Constraint C6 mandates exactly zeroed unused slots. The phase-mean form is therefore diluted by D / n_active.

The dual form is algebraically exact and mandatory: a D×D Gram at D = 65536 is 32 GiB in complex64 and 64 GiB in complex128, and the latter cannot be allocated at all on a 32 GiB card. Static memory scales as 16 M² bytes, so M=8192 costs 1 GiB and M=D costs the same as the dense form. The two costs cross where 9M³ = 2M²D, that is M ≈ 2D/9 ≈ 14.5k: below that the Gram product dominates, above it the eigen-solve does.

The “< 6 ms” claim is wrong by 127×. Measured 761.5 ms at M=4096. Newton-Schulz on the same operand measured 1007.0 ms over 15 iterations, so it is slower than eigh here, not faster. Any design that puts retraction inside a 50 µs loop is impossible on this arithmetic; retraction must stay on the cold path.

A finding the gate produced: the Margin Law is out of domain here

v6 MMLU telemetry gives t = 0.073207, c_max = 0.383656, margin = 0.029045. The law predicts t(1 - c_max) = 0.045121. Residual -0.016076, which is 3.5× the noise floor sqrt(ln 4 / 65536) = 0.004599.

The correction term assumes incoherent rivals. Here four rivals share most of their words, so c_max is 0.38 and the rival terms add instead of cancelling. The law is not violated; it is a large-M, low-coherence result and this is not that regime. Consequence: margin is not a usable confidence gate on option sets drawn from overlapping text. The Stage-8 result still holds, because there the codebook was orthogonalised first.
